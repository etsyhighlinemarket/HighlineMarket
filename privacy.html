Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what information the EtsyHighLineMarket website and posting tool ("the Service") collects and how it is used.

1. Information we collect

This website does not require registration and does not collect personal data from visitors. When the shop owner connects a TikTok account to the posting tool, we receive the TikTok display name and an access token that allows the tool to post on that account's behalf. We also store the videos, titles and captions that are scheduled.

2. How we use it

We use this information only to show which account is connected and to publish the scheduled content at the chosen time. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information. Content is sent only to the social media platforms that were selected, so that it can be published.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored on the owner's own computer or server. Access tokens are kept private and are never shown publicly.

5. Your choices and deleting data

You can disconnect TikTok at any time inside the Service, or remove access from TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and websites. Scheduled posts can be deleted inside the Service. To request deletion of all stored data, email etsy.highline783@gmail.com.

6. Children

The Service is not meant for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.

8. Contact

Questions? Email etsy.highline783@gmail.com.
