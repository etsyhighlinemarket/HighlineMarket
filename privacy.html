Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what information the EtsyHighLineMarket posting tool ("the Service") collects and how it is used.

1. Information we collect

When you connect your TikTok account, we receive your TikTok display name and an access token that allows the Service to post on your behalf. We also store the videos, titles and captions you schedule.

2. How we use it

We use this information only to show which account is connected and to publish the content you scheduled, at the time you chose. We do not use it for advertising or any other purpose.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. Content is sent only to the social media platforms you selected, so it can be published.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored on the owner's own computer or server. Access tokens are kept private and are never shown publicly.

5. Your choices and deleting data

You can disconnect TikTok at any time inside the Service, or remove access from TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and websites. You can also delete scheduled posts in the Service. To ask us to delete all stored data, email etsy.highline783@gmail.com.

6. Children

The Service is not meant for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

7. Contact

Questions? Email etsy.highline783@gmail.com.
